About SLiGO

Welcome to SLiGO, a comprehensive database of synthetic lethal (SL) interactions predicted using Gene Ontology (GO) annotations and synthetic network features. Synthetic lethality occurs when the simultaneous disruption of two genes leads to cell death, while disruption of either gene alone is viable. Our algorithm integrates multiple biological data sources to identify high-confidence SL pairs with potential therapeutic applications.

What can you do with SLiGO?

• Search for potential SL partners of your gene of interest across 6 cell lines and a general model

• Query specific gene pairs to retrieve confidence scores across all cell lines at once

• Filter single-gene results by confidence score and cell line

• Download results for further analysis

SLiGO Prediction Framework

SLxGO

General synthetic lethality prediction model providing genome-wide SL interaction predictions independent of cell-line context.

SLxGO++

Cell line-specific synthetic lethality prediction model enabling context-dependent analysis across HEK293, HeLa, A-375, A-549, JURKAT, and K-562 cell lines.

About This Project

The core idea behind SLiGO is to provide biologists and experimental researchers with safe, reliable, and high-quality predictions through a user-friendly platform. The website implements the SlxGO+ algorithm, which integrates SLant protein–protein interaction (PPI) information, Gene Ontology (GO) information, and gene expression data from the Human Protein Atlas. The model is designed to prioritize precision over overall accuracy, with the goal of minimizing false-positive predictions. This approach is particularly important for identifying synthetic lethal (SL) interactions, where reliable predictions can help researchers prioritize candidates for further experimental validation.
